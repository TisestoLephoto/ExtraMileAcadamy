About ExtraMile Academy

Going the extra mile for your education through quality, accessible and personalised academic support.

Our Story

ExtraMile Academy was established in 2023 to provide high-quality academic support to high school learners and help them reach their full academic potential.


The academy provides online tutoring for Grades 8 to 12, specialising in Accounting, Mathematics and Physics, with plans to expand its physical presence as the business grows.


Our approach combines subject expertise, classroom teaching experience and an understanding of the South African curriculum and assessment environment.

Our purpose

We believe tutoring should not simply be about helping learners pass. It should be about unlocking potential, pursuing excellence and equipping learners to achieve the best results possible.

Vision, Mission & Values

The principles that guide how we support learners.

Vision

To become a leading and trusted education support academy in South Africa, empowering learners to reach their full academic potential and creating opportunities for a brighter future through quality, accessible and personalised education.

Mission

To provide high-quality, personalised academic support through effective tutoring, targeted academic intervention and learner-centred teaching while building subject knowledge, confidence, critical thinking and independent learning skills.


Excellence

Accessibility

Making quality academic support available to more learners.

Personalisation

Integrity

Empowerment

Commitment

Going the extra mile for every learner.
